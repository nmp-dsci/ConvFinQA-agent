what moved

Program turns, +7.3 points

The DPO pairs are 80% program turns, and that's where the gain is: 70.1% → 77.4%. Number turns gain +4.6. The chosen answers lost likelihood (−9.5 nats), but the rejected lost more (−15.5) and val went up, so the displacement hasn't hurt.

caveats

Val picked it; test hasn't seen it

Val chose step 144 over step 72, so its 82.7% is slightly optimistic. Test 100, the student's holdout, is the unbiased number and opens only on your word (D1). One step-144 conversation hit a server 502 and was re-run whole with --resume-from, under the project's rule.

watch

Teacher chosens are twice as long

Mean chosen 1,533 student tokens against 706 for the rejected: DeepSeek's thinking becomes the visible reasoning block, as it did in SFT. DPO sums log-probs, so a longer chosen starts at a lower likelihood. The length-normalised NLL term holds it up. DPO v2 reports reward accuracy and log-prob change per source so a length artefact shows. Longest row 12.7k tokens (v1: 10.4k); v1 peaked at 14.5 GB of 24 GB.

on-policy-ish

Why a teacher chosen is reasonable

The student was SFT'd on exactly this writer's turns, so its style is what the student learned. The prompt is the student's own, so chosen and rejected share it byte for byte, the same as v1. Every row passes v1's row check and token-span check.
