DeepSeek can write a right trace from the gold answer that a judge mostly can't tell from a real one, but not quite well enough yet on program turns.

For questions nobody gets right, the builder asks the teacher again with the gold answer as a private note, keeps only traces that pass deterministic gates, and asks a Haiku 5.5 judge to pick the hinted trace out of a pair with a real right one. Over two hint wordings, the judge went from spotting the hinted trace 68% of the time (dev, round 1) to 54% (dev, round 2, the same as its chance level). On the sealed set, scored once, it spots it 58.7% against a chance level of 52.3%: +6.3 points, with a 95% interval of −0.7 to +13.6. That misses the pre-set bar (upper end ≤ +10). Number turns are indistinguishable; program turns still leak.

01

The approach: write with the answer, gate, then try to catch it

1 · write

DeepSeek Flash, told the answer

llm_react_v1 (the SFT teacher) is asked the question on its own conversation history, with the gold answer appended as a private note it must not mention. Its own harness and the six calculator tools, 2 tries a question. Off-peak DeepSeek.

2 · gate

Deterministic checks

G1 right answer (strict); G2 a program turn's answer is its own last tool result; G5 no reference to the note ("note", "hint", "hinted", "gold answer", "told", "expected answer"…); G6 no harness retry, and it renders as a DPO row.

3 · catch

Haiku 5.5 judge, two orders

The judge sees the history, the question and two right traces, and is told one agent was secretly given the answer: which? Every pair is judged A/B and B/A. Picking the same slot twice scores 0.5. It never sees gold or the note.

03

What still gives it away: the model talks about the number it was given

hint 1 tells

Verification against a known target

"Target: 5.8%", "expected −5.35", "Yes! Confirms.", "Change = 6.8. Confirmed." Hint 2 told the model not to state, check against or name the answer, which removed most of these and brought dev to chance.

sealed tells

Residual references, mostly program turns

The judge's 34 confident (≥ 0.8) calls on T were right 79% of the time; on C0, 50%. Its quoted evidence: "But target is 12388?", "given target 3.85", "the gold 1.23", "need derive 1.23", "even though known", "the answer expected 795786.9", "→ 12.4%. Matches."

Real traces use these words more often than hinted ones (the teacher talks like this anyway), so dropping hinted traces that use them doesn't plant a new tell. The words only betray a trace when they refer to the given number. Program turns keep a residual +6 even then. It comes from structure: operands chosen to hit a number, a check that "matches".
