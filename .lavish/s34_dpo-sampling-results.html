why 1

Fewer flips

s31's val passes put clean program turns at 17% flip and number turns at 9%. Here they are 13.2% and 7.1%. Val is harder than this pool, and 4 tries find fewer flips than 4 whole passes compared turn by turn.

why 2

One wrong try per flip

43 of the 87 four-try flips are 3 right and 1 wrong, so they can only give one pair. 76 of 88 flips have a single distinct wrong answer. Pairs per flip came out at 0.98, not the ≈ 1.6 the plan assumed.

why 3

15 flips unpaired

Round 1 pairs only clean tries. 175 tries (5.7%) needed a harness retry (H3 112, H2 55) or a schema correction (12), almost all on program turns. That leaves 15 of the 88 flips with no clean right or no clean wrong try.

finding

A quarter of the pairs are sign flips

In 22 of 86 pairs the rejected answer is exactly minus the chosen. The sign check also stopped 3 tries that numeric_match accepted with the wrong sign from becoming a chosen. Sign is the clearest single thing round 3 can teach.

finding

3 chosen answers are more than 2% off gold

Single_UNP (0.0931 for 0.0852) is wrong. Double_ZBH (4.2% for 4%) and Double_VTR (0.773 for 0.8) look like rounded gold. Rule 1 of numeric_match rounds both sides to an integer, so any two answers below 0.5 match. The pair labeller should add a relative tolerance, as it added the sign check (E2).

06

What I decided while building, and the gaps

  1. D1 · built straight away and D3 · 4 tries, as you chose on 11 Nov.
  2. D2 · the student's own right tries only. No Flash rescue. The 116 always-wrong questions give nothing.
  3. The harness stays unchanged. The retry-budget bug is still in, so the tries match what sft2-e1 was scored on. It cost 2 dropped tries out of 3,080.
  4. No sampler seed. vLLM's seed is null, so a re-run gives different tries. The tries are kept, so nothing needs re-running. Pairing and the kept try are seeded per question, so they reproduce from the record.
  5. Gap: no MLflow run. Sampling writes only to evaluation/dpo/v1/. A run with the manifest, sampler, prompt hash and stats should be logged before training, so the DPO run has a parent.
  6. Gap: not built yet. Pair rendering through sft/rows.py and the mask check, the top-up path, and tries.jsonl.gz. The record is ≈ 21 MB uncompressed at 300 conversations, so ≈ 105 MB for the pool. Gzipped, the 300 are 1.9 MB, so ≈ 9 MB for the pool.
