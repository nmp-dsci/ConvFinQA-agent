03 · Where they went wrong, mostly identically

These are the first wrong turn of every conversation either agent missed. On five of the six shared misses, Flash gave the champion's exact wrong answer. Two models reproducing each other's mistakes points at the prompt, or at the gold label, rather than at the model.
