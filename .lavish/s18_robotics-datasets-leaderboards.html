01 · The role

What the posting asks for, and which public task exercises it

Evaluation systems for quality, speed and cost
A judge-scored board (NVIDIA AlpaJudge) where you report the score together with the cost per clip and the time per clip. ConvFinQA's gate, McNemar test and MLflow loop carry over directly.
Retrieval over messy fleet data
Picking the right keyframes from a multi-camera clip, and searching across DROID episodes in natural language (Project C). MTEB covers the embedding side.
Inference cost down: routing, caching, distillation
NVIDIA Track 1 asks for 6 rollouts per event. Distilling a large VLM teacher into a small one, and routing easy events to the cheap model, is the whole game.
Task-specific models where APIs fall short
Fine-tuning a small time-series foundation model (TSFM) on BOOMLET, or a VLM on the PAI-AV train split.
Time-series and sensor data (nice to have)
BOOM: 350M points of real observability telemetry, the closest public stand-in for fleet telemetry.
Cross-fleet usage into datasets and detectors
NVIDIA Track 2 (auto-labelling), RoboFAC failure labels, and anomaly detection from forecast error on BOOM.
Open-source work they can read
Every project below ends in a public leaderboard row or a public repo, and each shows the portfolio's house UI.

04 · Projects

Three projects, each ending in a number someone else can check

A · do first · deadline

"Why did it do that?": NVIDIA AV reasoning, Tracks 1 and 2

  • Build: clip → keyframe and camera selection (retrieval) → open VLM (Cosmos-Reason2 / Qwen3-VL) → chain-of-causation → 6 rollouts, with the best one reranked into slot 1.
  • Loop: a local AlpaJudge proxy on the labelled train split, driving the same diagnose → rewrite → gate cycle as ConvFinQA.
  • Cost story: score against $ per event and seconds per event; distil the teacher into a small model; route events by difficulty.
  • Proof: a public board row before 31 Oct, plus a write-up of the cost curve.

Covers: evals · retrieval · cost · distillation · multimodal.

B · time series

Fleet telemetry: BOOM forecasting, then an anomaly detector

  • Build: baselines (seasonal naive, Chronos-Bolt, Toto) on BOOMLET → fine-tune a small TSFM → submit to BOOM.
  • Extend: turn forecast error into an anomaly score; inject faults into held-out series and report precision and recall.
  • Cost story: CRPS against inference ms per 1k series across model sizes.
  • Proof: a BOOM results PR, plus a detector benchmark in the repo.

Covers: sensor data · task-specific models · detectors.

C · the product

"Ask the fleet": natural-language search and failure reports over robot episodes

  • Build: index DROID episodes (video frames, state, language) and RoboFAC failures; an agent answers "show me grasps that failed on transparent objects" and writes a mission report.
  • Scoreboard: your own benchmark (recall@k, answer accuracy, $ per query), published as a Hugging Face dataset and Space, with PAI-Bench RoboFail as the external check.
  • UI: a new house-kit app: eight tabs, its own accent colour, make ui-new.
  • Proof: a public dataset, a Space and a leaderboard. This is the demo to show Alloy.

Covers: retrieval · agents · evals · the platform itself.

OrderA first, because its board closes on 31 Oct. B runs in parallel on CPU and cheap GPU. C starts once A's harness exists, because it reuses A's multimodal retrieval and eval code.

05 · Risks

What could waste the month

The judge is a modelAlpaJudge scores against hidden labels. Tuning to a local proxy can drift from it, so keep private submissions as the truth check; the board allows unpublished runs.
Compute284 events × 6 rollouts from a 7–32B VLM needs a GPU. Budget it: HF Inference Endpoints, a rented A100 for hours not days, or an API VLM as the teacher only. The platform runs no GPU today.
Gated dataYou have to accept the PAI-AV and Nexar licences in your own HF account. I didn't, and can't do it for you.
Crowded ceilingMany Track 1 entries tie at 0.857 on 7 Aug, which looks like a shared baseline. The gain comes from reranking and the cost story, not from a bigger model.
Where the code livesThe branch spike-multimodal-robotics sits on ConvFinQA-agent, but this work is not ConvFinQA. It probably wants its own sibling repo registered in nmp-central-ai, with a new accent colour and MLflow experiments named robotics/….
Not verifiedNexar's live collision board was on Kaggle, not Hugging Face, and I didn't check it. PAI-Bench does not accept self-serve submissions.
