s14 · design upgrade · branch design-upgrade · measured 8 Sep 2026 against the dev app at localhost:5173

The evidence is the best thing in this app, and the design hides it behind eleven unlabelled icons and 85 identical panels.

Every number on every page carries its denominator, its p-value and its provenance. But a first-time visitor lands on nine equal tiles, has to guess which of eleven icons means “how it was built”, reads most of the app at 10–11px, and is never told the one sentence the whole record supports: the loop took a four-agent pipeline from 77.1% to 81.7%, then a single Claude session reached 90.5% on the same sealed split — above the paper's 89.4% human figure — and the confidence judge tried afterwards made no measurable difference. The redesign is a presentation layer over the same data: a labelled story-shaped navigation, one type scale, one “star” per page, and the production gen-AI rubric shown in the product it is grading.

01 · substance before style

Who this is for, and the one thing they must leave knowing

The corpus rule (s20, stage 01): answer who / what action / objections / vibe before touching a layout. The app is a portfolio piece and a working tool at once, and the two audiences want the same spine in a different order.

Visitor · a technical reviewer, 90 seconds

Arrives from the portfolio's R1–R9 matrix or a CV link. Wants: what was built, how it was measured, whether the numbers are honest, and proof it runs. Objection: “a chatbot with a dashboard is not production engineering.” The answer is the loop, the gate and the scorecard — shown, not described.

Operator · you, in dev

Runs live turns on the Agent SDK against a filing, reads the trace, promotes or refuses a version, opens the judge's caution. Needs density and keyboard reach — the instrument-panel identity is right for this person and must survive the redesign.

Demo · the public URL

No key, replays eight recorded conversations, every write answers 403. The visitor must understand within one screen that the chat is a faithful replay of real traces and the admin pages are live against committed evidence — today that is a small dashed lamp and a banner most people will not parse.
